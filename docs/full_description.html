Speech recognition that runs on the TV. Nothing you say leaves it.

Hold the remote's microphone button, speak and let go. Your words go into the selected text box, or into a search on YouTube, and apps with their own microphone button get them too. A card at the bottom of the screen shows how loud your voice is.

Speech models are downloaded in the app, where you choose one. Moonshine tiny-en is about 30 MB.

Local Voice replaces LG's voice service while it is on, and gives it back when you turn it off. It needs root. After an update, turn it off and on again.

Looking for testers! Built on an LG BX with webOS 5.4. If you try it on another TV, please send a report from the app's Compatibility card. Reports are anonymous, and the results are on GitHub.
